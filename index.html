CJP Five Demands (Non-negotiable)

  • Jobs & Accountability: Real unemployment data and real action — not slogans on tax-funded billboards.
  • Education Reform: Full accountability on NEET paper leaks; scrap CBSE rechecking fees.
  • Press Freedom: A PM who faces real press conferences.
  • Judicial Dignity: Citizens are not cockroaches or parasites. Dignity is constitutional.
  • Transparency Now: Where did PM CARES funds go? Ask until answered.
